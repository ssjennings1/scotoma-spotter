  <h2>How I Got Here</h2>

  <p>I started out working with families in the hardest situations — divorce, custody, the stuff where somebody's kids are on the line. And what I found there is the thing I've never stopped finding: the problem people bring you is almost never the real problem. It's the structure around them. The unspoken rules. What everybody knows behind closed doors but nobody says in the hallway.</p>

  <p>I carried that into business at the Edward Lowe Foundation, running <a href="/how-i-work">peer retreats</a> for entrepreneurs and CEOs. Their whole mission was to champion the entrepreneurial spirit — and I'm still living that mission more than a decade after I left. Because these companies matter. The ones growing in your town, hiring locally, sponsoring the little leagues, putting money back in — they hold communities together, especially now, when a lot of other things are falling apart. That's who I've built my work around.</p>

  <p>Here's what I've learned in all those rooms: when you build something, you're in it. The business and the person aren't two separate things. Your money, your identity, your relationships, how you sleep — it's all tied up in the thing you made. That's true for the trades contractor and the manufacturer and the professional-services owner, and it gets even more tangled in a family business, where the succession talk is also a family talk and the org chart and the dinner table are the same table. You have to see the whole picture to be any real use in any of it. That's the work I love most.</p>

  <p>My doctorate in business psychology came later — all that studying put real structure around what 20 years of closed doors had already taught me. The APA Council seat means I get a say in where this whole field is headed, which I take seriously because it keeps me sharp. But the letters after my name aren't the point. The point is this: you can't fix what you can't see from inside it. My job is to be the person who can see it — and stay in the room until something actually moves.</p>

  <p>I'm based in Niles, Michigan, and I'm in rooms across the South Bend–Elkhart region — Kalamazoo to Fort Wayne — regularly. For everything else, I work virtually with owners across the country.</p>
