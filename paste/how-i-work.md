  <h2>What It Feels Like</h2>

  <p><strong>It doesn't matter what we call it.</strong><br>
  Coaching, advising, consulting, facilitation — those words get watered down. What matters is what happens when we work together. Here's what that looks like.</p>

  <h3>01 — You feel heard. Really heard.</h3>
  <p>Not managed. Not judged. I'm genuinely curious about what you're seeing, what you're carrying, and what you almost named but couldn't quite get to. I ask questions until the picture gets clear.</p>

  <h3>02 — You see something you couldn't see before.</h3>
  <p>Not because I told you what it was. Because together we decoded what was already there — connecting dots from things you've said, things your people have said, things that happened years ago that are still showing up today. And then you see it. And it's obvious.</p>

  <h3>03 — You leave with something to do Monday.</h3>
  <p>Insight without action is just a nice conversation. We don't stop there. Once the smoke clears, we figure out the next real move together. No 40-page deck. No jargon. Just what's next.</p>

  <h3>04 — You did it. Not me.</h3>
  <p>That's the part I love most and get the least credit for. The magic is yours. I just create the conditions for it to happen. You had this in you the whole time — you just needed someone to pull on the right thread.</p>

</div>
