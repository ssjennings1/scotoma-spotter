What Working With Me Looks Like — and What It Doesn't

The work is specific. So is who it's for.

This is:

  • A thinking partnership built around your real decisions
  • Direct, honest assessment — including the uncomfortable parts
  • Work that connects the business, the people, and everything tangled up between them
  • A long-term relationship when the situation calls for it
  • Grounded in 20 years of real work, in real rooms

This isn't:

  • A curriculum you work through on a schedule
  • Validation dressed up as coaching
  • Therapy. That's a different practice.
  • A retainer that runs past its usefulness
  • Theory from someone who's never been in the room
