Start With Your Team — leadership workshops, retreats, and strategy sessions

Facilitation is my favorite thing to do — and the easiest place to start.

The best way to find out if we're a fit is to get me in a room with your team. You don't have to commit to anything big. You provide the space and the snacks; I bring a session that's been run many times and works.

From there, it can grow into whatever the room actually needs:

  • Workshops — leadership development for your managers, built for the room you have, not a generic audience
  • Retreats — where the conversations that have been circling for months finally land, and the group leaves with decisions, not just a nice experience
  • Strategy sessions — where the team doesn't just talk about a problem, they build the solution together in the room, and walk out with something they can use
  • Speaking — a keynote for your conference, association, or peer group that doesn't feel like a lecture, and that people are still talking about on the drive home

What that's looked like:

  • A board that moved from working in the organization to actually governing it
  • A manufacturer's supervisor-development pilot that started as one session and grew into a company-wide rollout
  • A CEO peer forum that left with a decision instead of just a good conversation
