Who I Work With

You. You built something real. Now you're not sure you're running it right. You've gotten this far on instinct and sheer will, but somewhere along the way the way you've always done it stopped working — and you're carrying that alone. I've had this conversation with owners running $2M service companies and $40M manufacturers. The words are different. The weight is the same.

Your managers. Great at the work, still figuring out the leading. They got promoted because they're good — really good — but being good at the job and leading the people who do the job are different skills. Owners bring me in to help them build that toolbox and find their voice.

Your next generation. Stepping into something the family built is a lot. They're caught between what was built before them and what they want to build next, and they need someone who gets all of it — not just the business part.
