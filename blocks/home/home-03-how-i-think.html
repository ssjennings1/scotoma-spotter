How I Think

Here's the first thing worth knowing: this is a phase. Companies your size hit a stretch where what got you here quits working, and it's rough for everybody who goes through it — that part isn't about you. It's not a sign you're bad at this.

And here's the honest part: it is about you too. Getting through it means some of it changes with you — how you spend your time, what you hold onto, who you let in. That's not a knock. It's just the work. And it's a lot easier when you're not doing it alone.

I don't bring models for the sake of models. You're the expert on your business — your brain is already connecting everything you run into back to it. My job is to help you make the most of what you're great at and build systems around the rest.

One takeaway you can put into play right away. Otherwise, I haven't done my job.
