Who I Work With — owners and founders in trades, manufacturing, and family business

Owners and founders of companies that grew because you built them — trades, manufacturing, family businesses, professional firms. You got here by figuring it out, hiring people, and carrying more than anyone sees. You said yes to something you may not have known how to do — and you did it.

You might be here because:

  • You keep hearing you don't communicate well — and you're not sure what they want from you
  • Everything runs through you, so people keep things to themselves and wait
  • You quietly worry you're the emperor with no clothes
  • You keep hoping people will read your mind, and they don't
  • Your leadership team doesn't quite click, and you're the one holding it together
  • Someone you love is in a role they can't grow into, and everyone can feel it

If a few of those made you nod, you're in the right place.
